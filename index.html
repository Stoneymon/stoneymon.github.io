RoadGPT

A Program that takes Textual Descriptions of Roads and builds these 3D-Roads in BeamNG.tech to test autonomous driving agents.

RoadGPT is the Title of my Bachelors Thesis, which was published at the ICSE 2025 New Ideas and Emerging Results (NIER) Track and presented by my thesis supervisor Dr. Alessio Gambi.

Creating simulation test scenarios for autonomous vehicles had 2 main issues: existing approaches relied on complicated algorithms and heuristics, and they only created 2D-roads. RoadGPT aimed at solving these issues, adding the third dimension to testing scenarios and making the creation of testing scenarios accessible to everyone by taking natural language descriptions of roads and converting them to simulation scenarios.

The Flexcrash project is focused on the development of a flexible and hybrid manufacturing technology to produce tailored adaptive crash-tolerant structures made of green aluminium alloys.

With the IMC FH Krems Research Team, I developed a web application using HTML, JavaScript, Python, MPLD3, Flask and SQL to create road scenario simulations to gather driving and crash data.

EV Charging Demand Forecasting

A university capstone project in partnership with Siemens, where our team of 4 analyzed real-world charging station data from Seestadt Aspern to predict hourly electricity demand.

Starting from raw data, we handled the full pipeline: exploration, cleaning, and preprocessing. We then built and compared multiple forecasting models accounting for seasonal, weekly, and daily demand patterns.
The goal of this project was to optimize when to charge a local battery via solar panels, minimizing the power drawn from the grid by predicting peak demand before it happens.

Pixel Academy is a gamified Python learning experience built during the Heilbronn 2023 Blended Intensive Program, a cross-university collaboration between students from 3 institutions.

Working in a team of 6, we designed a Super Mario-style platformer where players explore levels collecting hint boxes that introduce Python concepts. Each level ends with a quiz: three pipes represent the answer choices, and you run your character into the right one.
